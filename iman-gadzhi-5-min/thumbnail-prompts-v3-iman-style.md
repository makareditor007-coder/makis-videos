# Thumbnail prompts v3 — built from Iman Gadzhi's real thumbnails

Uploads for every prompt: Image 1 = face photo, Image 2 = After Effects icon PNG, Image 3 = graph PNG.

## Patterns taken from Iman's channel (from the screenshots)
1. Face **centered**, big, chest-up, cut by the bottom edge, looking into the lens, **calm and confident** (never shocked).
2. **Photorealistic** studio photo with a cinematic grade, mostly a **dark navy-blue** backdrop. No flat illustration.
3. **One huge line of text behind his head**, partly hidden by it ("$1,950/Week", "$100,000", "$12,950/Month"), sometimes with a small **handwritten script accent** ("With AI", "a day").
4. **Objects on both sides of the face**: app icons held in his hands (Gemini/Claude), a small **white widget card with a green chart** ("$7,450 This month").
5. Few elements, everything big. No clutter.
