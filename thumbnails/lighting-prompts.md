## ADD-ON library (paste after "ADD-ON:")
**Emotions**
- `Expression: shocked, eyes wide open, eyebrows raised high, mouth slightly open.`
- `Expression: a confident half-smile, one eyebrow slightly raised.`
- `Expression: big genuine smile showing teeth, happy.`
- `Expression: skeptical, eyes slightly narrowed, head tilted a little.`
- `Expression: disappointed, lips pressed together, eyebrows pulled down.`

**Gestures** (always keep the last sentence: it prevents bad hands)
- `Gesture: my right hand raised at chest height, palm open and facing up, as if presenting something floating above it. Natural hands, five fingers each.`
- `Gesture: both hands raised at chest height, palms up, holding two invisible objects out toward the camera. Natural hands, five fingers each.`
- `Gesture: pointing with my right index finger to the empty space on the [left/right] side of the frame. Natural hands, five fingers each.`
- `Gesture: arms crossed over my chest. Natural hands, five fingers each.`
- `Gesture: one hand on my chin, thinking. Natural hands, five fingers each.`
- `Gesture: hands together in front of my chest, fingertips touching, explaining. Natural hands, five fingers each.`

**Framing** (if you need space for text on one side)
- `Framing: move me to the [left/right] third of the frame, keep the other two thirds empty.`
